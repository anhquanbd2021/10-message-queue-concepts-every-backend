Queue Guarantees Lab · toy broker

The flow is five concepts. The guarantees are the other five — and they break first.

Crash a consumer between the work and the commit and watch at-least-once redeliver the tail — then dedupe it. Park a poison message in the dead-letter queue. Outrun the consumer and watch queue depth climb to the wall. Read one topic from two consumer groups and learn that every group keeps its own bookmark.

Safe educational demo. Everything runs in your browser — no network calls, no real broker, no Kafka client. It's a model of the mechanics, not the infrastructure.

Delivery lab

At-least-once: crash between work and commit

10 payments, one partition, one crash — landing after the charge, before the offset commit. Restart redelivers the tail. A dedupe store keyed on partition:offset is what turns redelivery into exactly-once effects.

    The poison message: retry, retry, park

    job-4 carries a corrupt payload — it can never succeed. Below maxAttempts it is redelivered (a retry); at the cap it is parked so the partition behind it unblocks. Then someone has to drain it — a DLQ nobody drains is a graveyard.

      Backpressure lab

      When the producer outruns the consumer

      Queue depth grows by producer − consumer each tick. Past the alert line a human should already be paged; at capacity someone chooses the overflow policy — here the producer is blocked. Stall the producer and the backlog still takes ticks to drain.
